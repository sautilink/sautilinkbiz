Advertising, considered

Clear standards.
A better conversation.

Make your message truthful, respectful and easy to recognise as advertising. These standards apply whichever format you choose.

Read the Terms of Service
01 Say what you can stand behind.

Ads must be truthful, respectful and add value to the community. Misleading claims are prohibited. Review your creative and destination together so they tell the same story.

02 Make sponsorship clear.

Every advertisement must clearly display a Sponsored or Paid Promotion label. Help people understand who is speaking and why the content is being shown.

03 Know the content boundaries.

Prohibited categories include adult content, gambling, tobacco, weapons, hate speech and unapproved political campaigning.

A few helpful answers

Before you get started.

How do I start a campaign?

Send an advertising inquiry below with your business details and campaign goals. The business team will review your inquiry and discuss the next steps with you.

What should I include in my inquiry?

Include what you want to promote, who you hope to reach, your preferred format and your timeline. If you have a budget, creative or a destination link ready, include those too.

Can I discuss more than one format?

Yes. Describe the formats you are considering in your message. The examples above can help you explain the story you want to tell; the team can discuss the campaign details with you.

Where can I check creative requirements?

Start with the creative guide and advertising guidelines on this page. Ask the business team to confirm final asset specifications and placement requirements before preparing your finished files.

How are my inquiry details used?

Your inquiry details are used to respond to your request. Read the Privacy Policy for information about how personal data is handled.
